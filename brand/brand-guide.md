# Molar & Co. brand guide

Name is always written **Molar & Co.** (ampersand, capital M and C, period at the end).
Voice: warm, plain, reassuring. Short sentences. No jargon, no scare words.

## Logo
- **Mark:** a molar with two roots and soft highlights. **Lockup:** mark + "Molar" (Jost 500) over "& Co." (Jost 300, lighter teal).
- Files in `logo/`: `molar-co-lockup-light|dark`, `molar-co-icon-light|dark` (SVG + PNG), `facebook-profile-light|dark` (1024 square, safe for circle crop).
- Light variant on light grounds (white, mint, ground). Dark variant on `#14302F` or brand-800/900.
- Clear space: at least the width of one root around all sides. Minimum size: lockup 120 px wide, icon 24 px.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use the supplied SVG/PNG files | Retype the wordmark in another font |
| Keep teal on light, mint on dark | Recolor, outline, rotate, or add shadows |
| Use the icon alone when space is tight (avatars, favicons) | Stretch or squeeze it; put it on busy photos |
| Write "Molar & Co." exactly | "Molar and Co", "MOLAR & CO", "Molar&Co" |

## Fonts
- **DM Sans** (400, 500, 700): all app UI.
- **Jost** (300, 500): logo, social graphics, print. Never in the app UI.
